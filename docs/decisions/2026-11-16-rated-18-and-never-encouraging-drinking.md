# 2026-11-16 · Rated 18+, and never encouraging drinking

- **Decision:** Clinkr is rated 18+ on the App Store, by answering "Frequent" to alcohol references. It is presented as an app that helps you keep your drinking under control: fun, but for a good cause. Any message that could read as encouraging drinking gets reviewed before it ships.
- **Why:** The owner's call ahead of the first App Store submission. Counting drinks is the whole app, so "Frequent" is the honest answer, and App Store Review Guideline 1.4.3 rejects apps that encourage excessive drinking or appeal to minors.
- **Instead of:** Aiming for 13+ with "Infrequent", and the earlier position that nothing assumes the user wants to keep their drinking in check.
- **Also:** The first review changed three messages. The drink toast is now "Van 4." / "That's 4.", without "¡Salud!" / "Cheers!", which replaces that part of `2026-11-03-counts-are-friendly-facts-not-praise.md`. The water toast keeps "¡Agüita!". "Arrancar la noche" / "start the night" became "anotar" / "log" on the Watch's empty Tonight and the iPhone's empty History.
